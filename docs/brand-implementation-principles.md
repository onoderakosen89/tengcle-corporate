# Brand implementation principles

This note records implementation constraints for Site 2.0. It does not approve
new public copy or replace the master brand assets.

## Fixed hierarchy

- Master tagline: `think into the future`.
- Japanese supporting expression: `考える力を、ひとつの目的へ。`.
- Public explanation: think from multiple viewpoints about present value and
  problems, then develop the result into a form that remains useful over time.
- The Japanese line supplements and interprets the master tagline. It does not
  replace it.

## Meaning of “one”

`cle` does not mean making everyone agree or forcing reality into one answer.
It means retaining different viewpoints, context, tension, and uncertainty while
moving toward one shared purpose. Editorial work should distill the essential
point without discarding the complexity that makes it true.

Internal editing tests:

- Understanding may remain complex; the decision and expression must be clear.
- Distill, but do not discard relevant context.
- Avoid binary framing when the evidence supports more than two positions.
- Avoid language that performs intellectual superiority or difficulty.

## Purpose and evidence path

The common purpose is:

> まだ活かされていない価値を、人と社会に長く役立つ形へ育てる。

Every public explanation of “different perspectives, one purpose” must provide
a direct path to this statement. “Latent value” can include a place, business,
practical knowledge, technology, or relationship that has potential but is not
yet sufficiently useful. The action model is discovery → understanding → design
→ execution → continuity → verification.

Activity content may carry `latent_value`, `beneficiary`, `intervention`,
`outcome`, `durability`, evidence `source`/`date`, `tradeoffs`, `constraints`,
`action_stage`, and `status`. The schema requires an explicit
`publication_state`. Unknown outcomes and durability remain null or absent;
unpublished evidence must never be inferred into public copy. Copy review must
ask whether the common purpose is clear and whether each claim can be connected
to evidence.

The following are comparison candidates, not approved headlines:

- 複雑さを消さずに、本質を見つける。
- 異なる視点を、ひとつの目的へ。
- 複雑さに向き合い、未来につながる形へ。

## Logo boundary

The master brand is one unchanged symbol + `Tengcle` + `think into the future`
system across Japan, Hong Kong, and the United States. Until the approved vector
asset arrives, the website uses a text lockup placeholder:

- compact header lockup without the tagline;
- legal company name adjacent on company and legal surfaces;
- tagline-capable lockup in footer and brand context.

Do not generate a substitute logo, recolor it by region, or derive a production
asset from the reference PNG.
